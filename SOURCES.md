# Research and creative basis

Research checked 26 October 2026. Public profile state was inspected directly when search excerpts were incomplete. No private account information was used.

The trailer’s indexed caption also mentions Rayhan Gunawardena and Diyan Kinigama, but its full role breakdown was not accessible. Specific trailer directing credits were therefore omitted. An office address, booking phone number, email, client roster, commercial service list, release date, and awards were not verified and were not invented.

Other unrelated businesses called Biru Films appeared in search. The Indian-language YouTube channel `@BiruFilms` and Indonesian Askara Biru were excluded; the studio’s own Instagram link was used to identify the correct channel.

## Interpretation

Biru’s public identity and uploads suggest an emerging independent filmmaking practice. The website therefore focuses on original films and the creative notebook, rather than presenting a fabricated global commercial-production operation. This positioning is a design interpretation for the proposal, not a claim made by the studio.

## Creative references

- [Stink Films](https://stinkfilms.com/): project-led navigation and concise presentation of film work.
- [Somesuch](https://somesuch.co/): a film-first editorial structure with room for distinct creative voices.

These informed the emphasis on films and restraint in copy. Their layouts and assets were not reproduced.
